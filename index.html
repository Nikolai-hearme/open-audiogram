Screening, not diagnosis

Browser output is approximate until the exact device and headphones are acoustically calibrated. This demonstration cannot identify or rule out hearing loss. Seek a qualified hearing professional if you have concerns, sudden changes, pain, dizziness, tinnitus, or asymmetric hearing.

OpenAudiogram implements common audiogram conventions and a modified 10-down/5-up threshold search. Clinical programs must define calibration, environment, referral, consent, privacy, and regulatory controls for their intended use.
